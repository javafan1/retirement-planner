# Phase 6E-P3D3C4D0R — Survivor RMD and Roth minimal-change audit

## 1. Executive summary

**The Michigan smoke-test behavior is reproducible.** Generic deterministic execution leaves a deceased owner in the eligible owner set. The owner RMD calculator then applies that person's birth date to their original IRA, creating an actual debit and ordinary-income receipt. The same positive annual requirement stops scheduled Roth conversions. A synthetic older spouse born in 1960, dying in 2034, generated a $10,000 RMD in 2035 and stopped a previously funded $1,000 annual Roth conversion. The two-year-younger spouse reaches age 75 in 2037.

**Correcting the owner set alone leaves a second error.** Arkansas controlled and lifetime execution already exclude a deceased owner from future owner RMDs. Both omitted the successor balance from the survivor's 2037 RMD. The minimal safe design uses one immutable annual view to select the accounts in each eligible owner's RMD pool and debit those same original positions. A separate rule controls scheduled Roth stopping, and a later approval governs which successor assets can be converted. No annual execution loop, account records, tax formulas, or settlement pipeline need to be replaced.

The least disruptive first financial scope is a verified personal traditional IRA with a qualifying pre-RBD death, a valid spousal own-IRA assumption effective by the next calendar year, and an admitted state and execution mode. An on/after-RBD death-year obligation is a separate mandatory lane. Preserve typed refusal while its facts or state route are unresolved. This audit does not implement either lane.

## 8. Pre-RMD death design

For a verified decedent death before RBD in the initially supported scope, keep original positions invested in the household. From the approved own-election effective year, ordinary personal traditional IRA positions join exactly one living owner's IRA group. While both spouses live, each original owner controls their own IRA. A qualifying deceased younger spouse does not delay the older survivor's requirement. Use the survivor's applicable cohort, age, table and the actual prior December 31 balance of each now-eligible position. Preserve initial-year historical balance validation. For a survivor already subject to RMDs, their existing own group continues; only add successor positions in the admitted year. Zero prior December 31 successor balance yields zero contribution where no unresolved earlier obligation remains.

Begin with a death inside the modeled timeline and before the decedent's first distribution calendar year, election modeled on January 1 following death, at most one death, zero relevant basis, and a verified sole direct spouse with unlimited withdrawal right and no unresolved mandatory amounts. If spending or tax funding reaches the original IRA in the death year before the modeled election, require verified beneficiary treatment or refuse that event. Economic presence alone supplies no tax classification. This conservative period is narrower than federal law. Later coverage may admit pre-RBD deaths during the first distribution year with correctly specified history. ## 9. Death after RMD commencement

Separate the legal obligation, period paid, and recipient. The already-paid portion stays on its original taxable receipt, without a modeled new debit or extra cash. The death-year remaining amount belongs in an independent mandatory batch with the decedent obligation owner, age, prior balance and table, and beneficiary payee. It is not the spouse's ordinary own-IRA RMD or a Roth source. Initial election is modeled from the following January 1 only. Unverified unpaid amounts can block that election or the projection. Base federal inclusion uses the actual fully pretax payments once. State qualification for beneficiary amounts is independent.

## 11. Recommended immutable context

An immutable `AnnualRetirementOwnership` context is proposed, with a small resolver and separately validated `RetirementReceipt` evidence. These are design names. No balances are stored on the context. Per-year source entries carry original account identity/reference, original configured owner and type, effective date and RMD owner or obligation lane, verified qualification and unsupported reason. Effective date of death/ownership, cohort and relevant table proof are immutable inputs. Conversion rights are separate, default denied for successor sources until R4. Payee and actual event amount attach only to an executed receipt.

Existing account object identity remains the in-memory debit key. Treat it as a read-only handle and bind it to a frozen `AccountReference` for persisted evidence. Validate that any handle refers to exactly one current original position. Avoid mutable registry state and accidental deduplication of distinct investments. `RmdBalanceSnapshot` continues supplying prior December 31 amounts and `ProjectedPortfolio` current amounts. The verified account list is used by both `OwnerRmdService` and the allocator. Changing only one would size a larger survivor obligation and then either debit the wrong accounts or report a false shortfall.

R2a uses existing per-owner IRA and account-specific employer result shapes for ordinary years. In R2b, a separate mandatory batch or companion evidence keeps the decedent's obligation owner distinct from the beneficiary payee. Allocator, source and final record must agree about each obligation and original balance exactly once. Commit neither provisional cash nor a partly executed year if a gate fails.

## 14. Federal, Medicare and ending-value effects

Actual eligible pretax distributions and conversion amounts enter existing ordinary income and AGI once. An RMD already distributed before a partial start remains taxable in its actual period and does not become projected cash. Removal of a fictional RMD changes cash sources, taxable Social Security, AGI, funding withdrawals and effective tax, within current federal arithmetic. A successor election does not change filing status transitions or standard deduction. Additional Roth cash can raise MAGI, be bounded by funded federal bracket room, and affect account type and after-tax estate. A candidate with valid RMD cash can fund Medicare and tax first and retain the excess under existing rules. An actual conversion transfers equal source and destination dollars, with taxes paid separately.

The app's annual Medicare premium calculation uses the annual modeled federal MAGI and active participant count, then recalculates the year's spending and funding with the authoritative premium. R2/R4 must preserve this annual architecture and treat RMD/transfer evidence exactly once per pass. Real SSA generally uses historical tax information and permits a qualifying life-changing-event adjustment for death of spouse. Basis, penalties, nonqualified Roth, payout timing across tax years, and unsupported mixed-year splitting require separate tax rules and may refuse. Estate valuation continues to apply the modeled heir-tax rate to remaining deferred balances.
