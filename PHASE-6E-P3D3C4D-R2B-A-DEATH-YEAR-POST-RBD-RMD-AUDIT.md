## Executive summary

**PROPOSED_NOT_IMPLEMENTED.** The smallest safe R2b-B slice is a Florida Basic deterministic traditional IRA extension. Keep the January 1 modeled death and following-January treatment dates. Support a first-distribution-year death before the required beginning date (RBD), a modeled January death in the following year before the April 1 RBD, and a later modeled January death after RBD with a verified prior balance and no earlier payments in the death calendar year. In the post-RBD case, size the decedent's mandatory amount under the original owner's rules and debit it once as a distribution taxable to the surviving spouse. Beginning the following year, use either direct own-IRA election rules or the post-RBD beneficiary schedule.

Positive historical owner payments before a same-year death require chronology and opening cash evidence beyond the current January 1 convention. For a 401(k) rollover, closing the death-year obligation also leaves a separate question about the **rollover-year** beneficiary RMD. Recommend separate approval for those two extensions and for admitted employer-plan RBD facts.

## Verified current law and outstanding proposals

**VERIFIED_CURRENT_LAW.** SECURE Act §401 generally introduced the 10-year framework for applicable deaths after December 31, 2019, with transition exceptions. A surviving spouse is an eligible designated beneficiary under IRC §401(a)(9)(E) and §1.401(a)(9)-4. Beneficiary determination generally looks to the designated beneficiaries as of death and the September 30 determination date in the following year, subject to exclusions and other specific rules. The audit assumes one direct sole spouse, established facts and life-expectancy treatment. T.D. 10001 applies its amended RMD rules to distribution calendar years beginning January 1, 2025, and amended §1.402(c)-2 to distributions from that date. IRC §408(a)(6) applies similar minimum-distribution requirements to IRAs. ### Survivor routes

- **Own IRA.** Under §1.408-8(c), a direct sole spouse beneficiary with unlimited withdrawal rights can redesignate the account and is generally the IRA owner for the election year and later years. The own-IRA rule uses the survivor's cohort and applicable owner table. The special same-death-year election rule generally preserves the applicable decedent amount before treatment and avoids a second owner amount for that inherited position in the death year. The recommended following-January direct redesignation starts the survivor's own rules for that inherited IRA in the election calendar year, using its preceding December 31 balance. This is distinct from rolling money out of the inherited account. Check the late-election and catch-up requirements for alternate payout methods. [§1.408-8(c)–(d)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol6/xhtml/CFR-2025-title26-vol6-sec1-408-8.html)
- **Retained beneficiary.** For before-RBD sole-spouse life expectancy, the ordinary spousal delay can defer commencement until the later of the year following death and the year the decedent would reach applicable age. For on/after-RBD death, begin in the following year, using the greater of the survivor's current-year Single Life divisor and the decedent's Single Life divisor at death-year birthday age minus elapsed years. Retest the maximum annually. The life-expectancy comparison is separate from the decedent's death-year Uniform Lifetime divisor. Use the published exhaustion rules when applicable. Confirm employer plan terms allow the selected schedule and the separately approved discretionary payout assumption. [§1.401(a)(9)-3(c)–(d)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol6/xhtml/CFR-2025-title26-vol6-sec1-401a9-3.html) · [§1.401(a)(9)-5(d)–(e)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol6/xhtml/CFR-2025-title26-vol6-sec1-401a9-5.html) · [IRS beneficiary guidance](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/required-minimum-distributions-for-ira-beneficiaries)
- **Direct rollover of 401(k).** IRC §402(c) permits eligible spousal rollover. Required amounts are ineligible. Section 1.402(c)-2(j)(3) distinguishes death-year amounts and post-death calendar-year amounts. Death-year satisfaction is insufficient by itself for a following-January rollover. Pay the rollover-year source-specific beneficiary minimum first, then transfer the eligible remainder, with same-calendar-year sending and receipt. The receiving IRA's opening RMD valuation does not acquire a preceding December 31 balance for a transfer sent and received after that date. A transfer straddling calendar years uses different rules. The direct pretax rollover does not add a household cash receipt.
