## 5. Official RMD and legal validation

- **IRA election.** Treasury Regulation §1.408-8(c) permits a qualifying surviving spouse to treat the remaining IRA interest as their own when they are sole beneficiary with unlimited withdrawal rights. A named trust blocks this particular own-IRA election. Redesignation and permitted deemed elections have their own requirements. Unpaid catch-up amounts can limit a late election. The survivor generally follows owner rules in the election calendar year and later. The death-year special rule and actual beneficiary distributions require separate evidence. The R2a following-January date is an approved modeling boundary. [26 CFR §1.408-8](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol6/xhtml/CFR-2025-title26-vol6-sec1-408-8.html)
- **First distribution calendar year and deadline.** For personal IRAs the applicable age determines the first calendar year and the ordinary deadline is April 1 of the following year. A living owner's first calendar-year requirement does not move to a different obligation year because payment is deferred. IRS guidance distinguishes death before the required beginning date from death on or after it. The approved R2a lane is narrower and admits only deaths strictly before the first distribution calendar year, with mandatory history established. A death in the first distribution year, early in the following year before April 1, or after the legal required beginning date is out of scope. [26 CFR §1.401(a)(9)-2](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-1/subject-group-ECFR6f8c3724b50e44d/section-1.401(a)(9)-2) · [IRS Publication 590-B](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590b)
- **Age and divisor.** The final regulation sets age 75 for people born from 1960 onward. Its 1959 paragraph remains reserved in the reviewed eCFR, while existing Aledrix rules contain a legacy 1959 assumption. Preserve legacy results and refuse unsupported successor cohorts or tables. Uniform Lifetime Table III uses 24.6 at 75. A sole spouse beneficiary more than ten years younger can require the Joint and Last Survivor Table. The two-year-gap fixture qualifies for the ordinary divisor under the stated facts, while any live-owner table exception requires independent verification. Apply existing cent arithmetic to the allowed group of adjusted prior December 31 balances. Federal rules calculate each IRA's requirement and allow qualified aggregation for payment. With the same divisor and compatible adjustments, summing the balances before dividing gives the sum of the unrounded requirements. [26 CFR §§1.401(a)(9)-5 and 1.408-8(e)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol6/xhtml/CFR-2025-title26-vol6-sec1-401a9-5.html) · [IRS Publication 590-B, Tables II and III](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590b)
- **Applicable dates.** T.D. 10001 was published July 19, 2024 and effective September 17, 2024. The pertinent final RMD rules apply to distribution calendar years beginning on or after January 1, 2025, and the revised rollover regulation applies to distributions on or after that date. Use the reviewed version in synthetic future-year assumptions and verify later amendments before release. [T.D. 10001](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-07-19/html/2024-14542.htm)
