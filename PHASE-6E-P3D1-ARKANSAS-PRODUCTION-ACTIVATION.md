## Request ownership and presentation

1. On JavaFX, the controller freezes the input document and assigns a single-use request. The binding captures the owner thread, plan reference and UUID, source revision and projection epoch.
2. The bounded application analysis/history worker reconstructs a detached plan, including the financial mode and plan UUID. It runs the annual ledger and proof solvers, captures schema three, validates reconstruction and prepares summary, assets and chart data.
3. The JavaFX callback checks the current request, owner thread, plan identity, document, revision and epoch before publishing. Cancellation, newer requests, edits, swaps, file operations and shutdown revoke the old request. Cancel closes feedback immediately. Worker cancellation checks occur before input reconstruction, at annual, funding and targeted Roth boundaries, and before handoff.
4. The delayed modal shows completed years and Cancel. JavaFX continues processing events while it is visible. Progress and failure callbacks check the active request before updating the window.
5. Successful completion installs its own immutable UUID and annual evidence. Failure and cancellation retain previous accepted numbers and history. A known funding shortage sets the ordinary funding outcome. Unsupported Arkansas tax carries the tax year and typed explanation and never supplies a substitute tax or a completed-year prefix to the Summary.

The Arkansas warning reuses the State Tax Unavailable dialog. It distinguishes pension origin, retirement arrangements, timing, additional tax, basis, NIIT, low-income coverage, policy and funding or Roth proof limits. It includes bounded detail from the failed year. Review Pensions resolves the original live row only while the failed request still identifies its owner, revision and input document. The existing editor preserves the independent Alabama DB/DC classification. Apply marks the plan modified and starts a new Arkansas request. Cancel leaves the row and accepted result intact.
