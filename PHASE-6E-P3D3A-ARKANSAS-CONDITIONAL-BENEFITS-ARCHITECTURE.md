# Phase 6E-P3D3A — Arkansas conditional benefits and life insurance architecture

Audit date November 20, 2026. Source baseline `2bedb55e99fb48aa31225e9c5549c842d77ebb2d`.

## 1. Executive summary

**Recommend reusing the existing conditional-benefit evaluator, typed life-principal receipt, and household cash settlement.** Aledrix already models ordinary personal life insurance principal as a one-time survivor receipt in supported jurisdictions. Arkansas has two independent barriers. The plan gate refuses every nonempty conditional-benefit list that reaches it, and the annual Arkansas reconciler rejects the life-principal exclusion even when a valid receipt reaches that layer directly. The state has a verified legal basis for excluding ordinary death principal. Release admission still needs financial and historical proof.

The smallest first release pays only verified personal-policy principal, once in the modeled death year, to the other living household member. Gross proceeds are available for the year's spending, Medicare and tax funding. Only the unspent household cash enters the existing projected retained nonqualified balance. Growth on that balance begins the following year. If a named cash or brokerage account is a required first-release feature, it needs a separately approved settlement foundation. The existing destination is a household pool with no account ID or individual title.

Fully taxable date-, age-, or death-triggered payments already have event and ordinary-income plumbing. Arkansas admission for them is a separate decision. Basic mode is the first candidate. Advanced NIIT currently refuses generic taxable conditional receipts because their source character is unresolved.

## 3. Arkansas and federal legal authorities

- **Federal income tax.** IRC §101(a) ordinarily excludes eligible amounts paid by reason of death under a life insurance contract. §§101(a)(2)–(3), (c), (d), (f), and (j) address transfers and reportable policy sales, interest, deferred payments, certain flexible-premium contracts and employer-owned contracts. §7702 defines a life insurance contract for federal tax purposes. Treasury regulations and IRS instructions describe beneficiary and estate treatment and the installment split. ## 6. Death and survivor event ordering

1. `EffectiveHouseholdDeathView` resolves configured or scenario deaths to January 1. The insured is deceased throughout that year. In couples the filed status assumption applies through first death year and the surviving filer switches to Single later. Arkansas reconciles active recipient facts separately from annual filing status.
2. The Social Security provider builds year-indexed amounts before the loop. Supported modern plans use monthly elections, January death cutoffs and survivor commencement under the applicable election and eligibility rules. A survivor amount depends on the election and age. Unsupported legacy shapes use a separate path. Pension eligibility and any elected pension survivor payment use the effective death view annually, with the first-year projection date and later year-end references. Pension and Social Security remain separate from conditional benefits.
3. Each year selects immutable conditional receipts. The calculation computes growth on opening balances, determines guaranteed cash and expenses, calculates opening RMDs with opening evidence or subsequent RMDs from prior December 31 projected balances, applies growth, executes required withdrawals, and funds residual spending. Contractual premiums stop when the insured is deceased. Other expenses use their own activity and survivor adjustment rules.
4. Roth executes from the post-RMD, post-spending portfolio. Tax trials include the actual conversion, annual sources and funding withdrawals. Medicare coverage is annual for modeled living age-eligible people. A provisional calculation determines the annual premium, then a premium-aware calculation reruns from the same opening portfolio and receipt list. Final cash settlement and ending balances are published once, and the ending balance feeds the next RMD snapshot.

## 8. Federal, Arkansas, Social Security, NIIT and IRMAA classification

- **Reuse typed classifications.** Keep `FULLY_TAXABLE_ORDINARY_NON_PENSION`, `QUALIFIED_LIFE_INSURANCE_DEATH_PRINCIPAL_EXCLUDED` and the separately admitted nonqualified annuity basis classification. Excluded principal is a positive cash receipt with zero taxable amount and excluded amount equal to gross. An Arkansas admission rule should validate the definition and the annual receipt against the frozen definition and scenario, then identify the Arkansas exclusion independently. Invalid, missing or unsupported character stops the requested execution. A generic tax toggle, user label or taxable amount of zero cannot establish a statutory exclusion. Drafts with unsupported recognized definitions can still be preserved. Unknown serialized tokens follow the existing reader's rejection rules.
- **Federal and Social Security paths.** `IncomeSourceAttribution.conditional` preserves the person, year, kind, gross and taxable receipt facts. `TaxIncomeAttribution.attach` sums the taxable portion into conditional ordinary income. `TaxIncome` includes it in ordinary income before Social Security. Federal tax uses these same base facts for Social Security inclusion, AGI, deduction and brackets. Qualifying principal directly adds zero to provisional income and federal AGI. Gross Social Security entitlement still comes from the authoritative household strategy result. Even a fully taxable receipt may affect the taxable portion of federal Social Security. Arkansas reconciles federal AGI and subtracts taxable Social Security. Its retirement exclusion for otherwise qualifying sources is independent of ordinary conditional income. Neither life principal nor generic ordinary conditional income enters the Arkansas pension/IRA exclusion pool.
- **Medicare.** IRC §101 death principal is outside AGI and is not tax-exempt interest. It has no direct IRMAA MAGI effect. Taxable conditional income or interest can increase AGI. Changed IRA withdrawals and conversions can change MAGI indirectly in either direction. Real-world SSA generally uses an earlier tax return, commonly two years before the premium year. The existing projection uses modeled same-year federal AGI and annual coverage in its premium-aware cash-flow pass. Preserve and disclose that planning approximation. This phase supplies no IRMAA appeal, lookback or MAGI redesign. [SSA 2026 Medicare premium guidance](https://www.ssa.gov/benefits/medicare/medicare-premiums.html) · [Social Security Act §1839(i)](https://www.ssa.gov/OP_Home/ssact/title18/1839.htm)
- **NIIT.** Excluded principal is outside net investment income and has no direct MAGI contribution. Taxable investment interest and annuity earnings can enter NIIT, subject to §1411. Proving a fully taxable receipt is ordinary income does not identify whether the source is investment income. Even verified non-investment receipts may raise MAGI and expose other investment income to NIIT. Basic mode omits NIIT. Advanced preflight refuses generic taxable conditional definitions and uncertified source shapes. Its annual NIIT calculator can accept a zero-taxable conditional receipt, but later nonzero growth on retained proceeds hits the existing unclassified nonqualified-growth guard. Recommend initial Arkansas benefit admission in Basic mode, with Advanced subject to a separately evidenced and approved subset. No automatic mode changes. [26 USC §86](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s86/) · [26 USC §1411](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s1411/)

Preserving the existing annuity evidence is preferable to a catch-all mixed-payment or tax-deferred toggle. Unknown classification is an admission failure. State modules own what they admit, even when the event evaluator is shared.

## 9. Premiums

A bounded insured-contingent premium already exists. Build on `ContractualPremiumPolicy.INSURED_ALIVE_FIXED_NOMINAL_MONTHS_V1` and the dedicated expense control in `ExpenseDialog`. The model charges fixed nominal premiums for inclusive active months while the named insured is alive. The existing first-year/opening month convention applies. January 1 death yields zero for the entire death year and thereafter. The dedicated expense branch precedes the general escalation, survivor multiplier and stochastic adjustment branches. Premiums are household spending with no automatic tax deduction or internal valuation. A generic recurring expense can stop on a user-specified date, but does not by itself switch off for the death of one specified person while the other survives.

## 10. Preventing insurance asset double counting

- **Face amount** is a contract descriptor for future payout. Pure term-life face value contributes zero to investable assets before death. The conditional benefit amount is the expected eligible principal paid while in force and can differ from face value.
- **Cash surrender value** is a different realizable contract value. The current personal death-receipt model has no cash-value asset or disposition event. Generic non-investable assets are projected independently, so entering whole-life face or surrender value there creates a second valuation stream with no automatic removal at death. First-release disclosure and validation should require whole-life payout-only treatment and keep the same contract out of opening investment and non-investment balances. Future policy-value support would require attributable basis and a linked extinguishment or disposition.
- **Received proceeds** enter the household once. Pre-opening proceeds already in a beneficiary's assets are represented in opening balances. An unpaid prior claim is outside scope. Coverage-date and one-time evaluation prevent replay. Ban parallel windfall entries for the same payout by instruction and explicit validation where a structured linkage exists. A pair of different, genuine policy IDs may pay on the same death. Two records for the same contract cannot reliably be recognized from labels.
- **After receipt**, report the actual balance remaining after settlement and subsequent growth. Do not add a receivable or full payout again to estate. Aledrix's after-tax investable estate is a balance metric with a modeled tax-deferred haircut. It excludes non-investable assets and does not compute federal gross estate, inheritance tax, probate property, trust distributions or ownership transfers.
