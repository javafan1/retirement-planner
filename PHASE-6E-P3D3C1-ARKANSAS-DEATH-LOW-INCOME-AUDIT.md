## 1. Finding and scope of reproduction

The 2028 message identifies the existing Arkansas low-income **admission guard**. An exact tax trial established a positive regular Arkansas estimate, then failed to prove that both the low-income schedule and the additional credit were immaterial. This guard and its ceiling rules are present at committed HEAD, before P3D3C. The same refusal was reproduced using an Arkansas Basic death scenario with no life policy. A second synthetic comparison showed that a qualifying payout can also expose the guard by replacing otherwise taxable IRA spending withdrawals. A third reproduction isolated a refusal at a preliminary funding trial even though the minimum funded outcome was independently calculable under the reviewed Arkansas policy.

The reported native message establishes the guard and the tax year. No matching native saved-plan copy or trial trace was available to this audit. The native report alone does not identify the configured death year, which eligibility bound failed, or whether the failed trial belonged to fixed funding, a Roth target, or a Medicare pass. The comparisons below isolate those possibilities without making claims about the private plan. The native acceptance account reported preservation of the preceding projection. ## 3. Arkansas law and the treatment of exempt proceeds

| Question | Verified authority and application |
|---|---|
| **§26-51-301 low-income exemption and schedule** | The statute establishes filing-category gross-income limits, a phased credit and annual capped cost-of-living adjustments. Subsection (g) uses income from all sources for credit eligibility regardless of Arkansas taxability. DFA applies the all-source qualification to the low-income tables. Once eligible, the table lookup uses **Arkansas AGI on line 25**. Married taxpayers qualifying for the table file jointly. Claiming specified military, employment-pension or qualified-IRA exemptions, or itemizing, disqualifies the route. DFA allows foregoing relevant exclusions to use the table if otherwise eligible. The table incorporates the standard deduction and the 2025 instructions direct a zero on line 27 when using it. ## 5. Boundaries, Roth and exact funding trials

- **Only the gross bound can be excessive.** A direct 2028 MFJ annual case with validated $100,000 life principal and $38,000 ordinary taxable interest has `Glo=$38,000` and `Nlo=$33,060`. The existing calculator refuses. Counting the documented receipt toward the eligibility measure gives an independently defensible gross lower bound of $138,000. Both ceilings then clear. The modeled regular liability would be $798.02. This demonstrates a narrow candidate for improvement without a new low-income worksheet. At $35,140 ordinary interest, net is exactly $30,200 and still fails even with the improved gross evidence. At $35,140.01, net clears. The existing gross guard refused at $39,292 and passed at $39,292.01, with $845.82 state tax at the passing point. In Single interest-only controls, $32,670 failed at net exactly $30,200 and $32,670.01 passed with $721.20 state tax. Gross was already above the Single bound for both net-boundary points.
- **Roth can change the outcome.** With $30,000 survivor pension, one saturated retirement pool, $100,000 in life proceeds and no ordinary obligations, fixed conversions of $0 and $10,000 refused. At $10,000, gross is $40,000 and net is $29,060, so §501 alone prevents admission under current bounds. A fixed $12,500 conversion produces taxable net $31,560 and passes with federal tax $1,030 and Arkansas tax $742.52. With enough capacity the 12% target completes at $103,000 in conversion and $133,000 AGI. Limiting otherwise identical conversion capacity to $10,000 yields `TARGET_PROOF/LOW_INCOME`. The last outcome establishes a target-path refusal. Its own top trial does not establish that a lower target candidate is certifiable.
- **A funding trial can block an otherwise certifiable final outcome.** With $30,000 survivor pension, $100,000 life proceeds, $130,000 spending and a fixed $11,000 conversion, the zero tax-withdrawal trial has `A=$41,000`, `Nlo=$30,060` and no other tax cash.
