# Phase 6D.4 — Additional no-general-individual-income-tax states

## Executive summary and admission gate

Admission reviewed October 13, 2026, before activation. Audited and admitted:
Alaska (AK), Nevada (NV), New Hampshire (NH), South Dakota (SD), Tennessee (TN),
Texas (TX), Wyoming (WY). No candidate required a specialized income-tax calculator.
Washington was expressly excluded, not evaluated as Florida-equivalent. California,
Iowa, Mississippi and all other jurisdictions remain disabled.

The eight requested predecessor reports were reviewed, together with the production
registry, immutable assumptions/request/result, Florida adapter, Pennsylvania Route A
guard, attributed income, annual funding and bracket-fill calls, RMDs, integrated
analyzers, Monte Carlo, shared wizard/Assumptions control, persistence and exports.
Existing-state financial rules and expectations are frozen.

Stage A established each state's ordinary individual-income boundary from official
government material. The category conclusions below are inferences from the absence
of the applicable individual income tax, not claims that every state publishes a
separate tax worksheet for every retirement transaction. Stage B registers only the
seven admitted candidates. Research JSON remains the original eight-state catalog;
it is not the production activation registry and was not expanded or changed.

### Historical repeals

New Hampshire: RSA 77 no longer imposes the former interest/dividends tax for taxable
periods beginning after December 31, 2024. The 2023 acceleration superseded the older
phaseout schedule. 2024 obligations can still require historical filing; they do not
become a 2026 tax. Explicit tests use large interest and ordinary/qualified dividends
and expect calculated zero in 2026.

Tennessee: the Hall tax formerly addressed bond/note interest and stock dividends.
DOR says not to file Hall returns for periods beginning January 1, 2021 or later.
Earlier phaseout rates are not carried into 2026. A named regression verifies the
repeal independently of general registry parameterization.

## Production architecture

All Java paths are relative to src/main/java/com/daviddunn/retirementplanner.

~~~mermaid
flowchart TD
    P[Projection / funding / Roth trials / analyzers / Monte Carlo] --> S[ProductionStateTaxService]
    S --> MI[Unchanged Michigan adapter]
    S --> IL[Unchanged Illinois calculator]
    S --> PA[Unchanged Pennsylvania bounded calculator]
    S --> Z[One stateless NoGeneralIndividualIncomeTaxCalculator]
    Z --> J[FL / AK / NV / NH / SD / TN / TX / WY]
    J --> R[Requested jurisdiction + CALCULATED zero]
    PA --> U[Existing typed UNSUPPORTED / Route A]
~~~

The production enum carries a small admitted-model descriptor, not a tax rate or an
assumed default for unknown jurisdictions. The registry maps the eight explicitly
admitted zero-tax jurisdictions to one calculator instance. No state branches were
added to projection, funding, optimizer, analyzers, simulations or their statistics.
The retained Florida adapter delegates to this implementation for compatibility;
the live registry uses the shared strategy directly.

Florida's result amount, status, method and limitation text remain exactly unchanged.
New results carry the actual jurisdiction/name, validated year 2026, rule version
no-general-individual-income-tax-2026-v1, modeled year and scope/current-law warning
in the existing result diagnostics. ProjectionYear retains the actual annual year.
No result/persistence schema redesign was required.

New states reject pre-2026 requests with UNSUPPORTED_MODEL_RULE and no tax amount,
not historical calculated zero. Future years project the validated baseline forward
without forecasting law. Florida's existing historical compatibility is preserved.
Current-law extrapolation is explicit, not a claim that any state will never tax income.

No JSON parsing, resource loading, reflection, mutable shared result or tax-law API
is introduced in annual calculation. Rules/strategy selection remain offline,
deterministic and thread-safe.
