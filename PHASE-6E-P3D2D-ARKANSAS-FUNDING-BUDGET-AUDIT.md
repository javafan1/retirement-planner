# Phase 6E-P3D2D — Arkansas deterministic funding candidate budget audit

Audit date November 20, 2026. Aledrix Retirement Planning, Java 25 on Windows.

## 1. Executive summary

The two reported failures indicate an exhausted **exact funding candidate count**. In the supplied stack, the exception surfaced through the Roth-target branch. The budget that emitted the message belongs to one inner funding pass, with a production maximum of 512 candidates. A target run can reduce that pass's allowance to the remaining portion of its separate 4,096-candidate total. The log does not record the effective allowance, failed conversion trial, or original annual inputs.

**A synthetic 2028 projection reproduced the same message, typed issue, and line 96 target branch.** The fixture had enough cash and an independently verified conversion and funding solution. One documented pension already filled the recipient's Arkansas exclusion pool. Another pension had Unknown origin. Exact tax was stable across both classifications, but the interval-bound code compared the uncapped receipt totals and declined to bound the search. The first target trial used all 512 exact funding candidates. The same fixture also exhausted the budget with zero or fixed Roth conversion on the direct funding branch.

The original plan was not identified and its exhaustion mechanism remains unverified. Recommend preserving the budgets and certifying a narrow improvement that recognizes when the known and possible receipts already fill the same recipient-level exclusion cap. This would address the demonstrated redundant proof work without assigning a pension origin, broadening tax admission, or changing annual tax formulas. Phase 6E-P3D2E requires approval.

## 5. Search algorithm and complexity

The inner solver finds the **first sufficient executable amount**. Other tax cash is remaining guaranteed receipts and projected RMD cash after expenses and Medicare, floored at zero. The exact test is `otherTaxCash + executableCash ≥ rawFederalTax + modeledArkansasTax`. Arkansas rounds on its approved cents basis. The solver does not round away a positive raw federal deficit. Only the selected sufficient trace settles the projected ledger. [ArkansasAnnualFunding.java:39–59](src/main/java/com/daviddunn/retirementplanner/domain/tax/ArkansasAnnualFunding.java#L39-L59) · [ArkansasAnnualFunding.java:140–176](src/main/java/com/daviddunn/retirementplanner/domain/tax/ArkansasAnnualFunding.java#L140-L176)

1. Price zero. If insufficient, partition positive cent amounts at cumulative retained-cash and account balances in the approved withdrawal order. Each segment fixes the marginal source characteristics. The allocator pays from retained nonqualified assets first and then from the ordered accounts. It neither searches account permutations nor chooses a cheaper arbitrary account. ## 10. Search efficiency findings

The demonstrated inefficiency is **missing certified pruning**, not repeated exact attempts at the same amount. Unresolved pension origin increases `possible` but leaves `certain` alone. Exact income tax already bounds each recipient's selected exclusion to $6,000. In the synthetic case, that person's $24,000 certain and $84,000 possible receipts have identical effective exclusion and known age. The state interval code declines the proof on raw inequality before the worksheet ever applies the cap. A single recipient's guaranteed exhaustion of the pool can make those differences irrelevant over the entire admitted source-homogeneous interval. The spouse's pool is separate. - Reuse exact-left reconciliation within a pass during repeated bound attempts. Hold the verified input and calculation policy constant, keep the full exact evidence check, and compute the interval-specific bound separately. ## 11. Roth conversion interaction

A schedule can request a fixed amount, a published-bracket ceiling or a custom taxable-income target. Actual execution uses available sources/destinations in the converter's documented owner order after RMD and spending. The tax-funding allocator then uses its separate withdrawal order on the post-conversion portfolio. Conversion contributes taxable ordinary income without spendable cash from the converted principal.
