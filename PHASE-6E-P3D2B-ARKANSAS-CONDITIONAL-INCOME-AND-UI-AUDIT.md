# Phase 6E-P3D2B — Arkansas conditional income and projection-failure UI audit

Audit date November 20, 2026.

## 1. Executive summary

**The projection refusal is an intentional release boundary. The failure presentation is a confirmed UI defect.** Arkansas admits ordinary production projections, but the plan-level Conditional Benefits gate refuses a plan with any entry in that list once earlier checks pass. The gate does not inspect whether a payment falls in the projection horizon or whether its tax treatment was explicitly declared. Lower-level Arkansas code can reconcile some receipt types. That is useful evidence for a future admission review, but it does not establish that those plan configurations are certified through the production funding, Roth, and result-validation routes. ## 6. Arkansas tax classification and policy

The reviewed Arkansas gross-income rule generally includes realized ordinary income except return of capital or an applicable exclusion. Its constructive-receipt provisions depend on whether money is available without substantial restriction. A future date, age or death condition does not itself establish an exclusion or establish that the cash-flow date always determines tax timing. The November 20 review verified the Arkansas rule and reviewed the DFA forms listing, the final tax-year 2025 annual instructions, and the 2026 special-session Act 2. No final tax-year 2026 AR1000F/AR1000NR instructions or annual tables were verified in the accessible sources. The 2026 AR1000ES packet is estimated-payment guidance.
