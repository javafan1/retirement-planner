## 6. Pension-source preflight

The existing worksheet remains the materiality authority. For a material ordinary pension-origin ambiguity, the annual tax diagnostic records the eligible recipients with room in their pool. Preflight checks authoritative annual payments and explicit evidence, then maps once to editable pension ordinals with name, original owner and actual recipient. The list excludes recipients with a filled pool, ineligible or absent receipts, and independently substantiated records. Several unknown receipts for the same unresolved pool may require review. Tax-stable zero and other provably equal results avoid review. Private contracts and special arrangements remain separate unsupported categories.

## 7. Review Pensions navigation preparation

Before navigation the controller revalidates the failed request's exact active inputs, plan identity and revision. It resolves the live row on the owner thread. Apply marks the plan modified and asks for a new projection, without running the ordinary synchronous refresh or altering prior history.
